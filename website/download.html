Download

You need two things: the app on your phone, and the free desktop app on your computer.

Beta

iPhone

Pocket Webcam for iPhone, in beta on TestFlight. Requires iOS 17 or later. Free on the App Store at launch.

Get a beta invite
Preview

Linux

Desktop app with a virtual webcam (v4l2loopback), for 64-bit PCs. Tested on Arch Linux; the Ubuntu, Debian and Fedora packages are new and not tested on those systems yet. Linux setup guide.

Ubuntu / Debian (.deb, 20 MB) Fedora (.rpm, 29 MB) Arch and others (.tar.gz, 29 MB)
Preview

Windows

Desktop app with a virtual webcam for Windows 11. The phone’s microphone isn’t on Windows yet (why). The installer isn’t signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows setup guide.

Download for Windows (33 MB)
Later

Android

Pocket Webcam for Android (Android 10 or later) comes after the iPhone launch. It uses the same desktop app as the iPhone version.

Join the Android beta

What you’ll need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later (Android phones: later)
  • A computer running Windows 11 or Linux
  • Both on the same Wi-Fi network

On a Mac? macOS already has Apple’s Continuity Camera built in, which lets you use your iPhone as a webcam for free.

Coming from another app? Compare Pocket Webcam with Camo, EpocCam and iVCam.

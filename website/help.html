Help

Most problems come down to the network. Start at the top.

My computer can’t find my phone

  • Same network. Phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks, hotel and office Wi-Fi often block devices from seeing each other.
  • Local network permission. On your iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → turn on Pocket Webcam.
  • Keep the app open. Phones pause the camera and network when the app is in the background or the phone is locked.
  • Firewall. Your computer must allow mDNS (UDP port 5353) for discovery, and outgoing connections to the phone on TCP port 7310.
  • Connect manually (once the phone is paired). In the phone app, look for the phone’s address, for example 192.168.1.42:7310 (in Settings on iPhone, on the main screen on Android). On Linux, run: 
    pocketwebcam --host 192.168.1.42

Pairing doesn’t work

Your phone and computer pair once. After that, only paired devices can connect, and everything between them is encrypted.

  • How to pair. Open Pocket Webcam on both, on the same Wi-Fi. The desktop app shows a QR code (or choose Pair a phone). On the iPhone, tap Pair with a computer and scan it.
  • Can’t scan? Tap Can’t scan? Pair with a code instead. Both screens show a 6-digit code: pair only if they match. If they don’t, cancel, because something else answered.
  • It used to connect, now it doesn’t. If you reinstalled either app or chose Forget, the pairing is gone on that side. Pair again.
  • The desktop app can’t pair with an old phone app. Pairing needs Pocket Webcam 0.2.0 or later on both. Update the phone app in TestFlight and the desktop app from the download page.
  • Remove a pairing. iPhone: Settings → Paired computers. Desktop app: Settings → Paired phones → Forget.

“Pocket Webcam” doesn’t show up as a camera

Linux

  • The virtual camera needs the v4l2loopback kernel module. Check that /dev/video10 exists.
  • Chrome and other browsers only list it with the exclusive_caps=1 option, which the setup script enables.
  • Start the desktop app before opening your video app, or restart the video app afterwards.

My phone’s microphone doesn’t show up

  • Linux: pick Pocket Webcam Microphone as the microphone in your video app.
  • Windows: not available yet. Windows needs a signed driver for a new microphone, and signing costs money every year. It’s the next thing donations pay for: support Pocket Webcam. Until then, use your computer’s own microphone.

The video is choppy or delayed

  • Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi if your router has it, and keep the phone close to the router.
  • Lower the quality or resolution in the app. 1080p at 30 fps is plenty for video calls.
  • Try the HEVC codec in the app’s settings: same quality with less data.
  • Other heavy traffic on the network (downloads, streaming) competes with your video.

My phone gets hot

  • 4K and high frame rates take a lot of power. For calls, 1080p at 30 fps is the sweet spot.
  • Use the app’s dim-screen option while streaming.
  • Take the phone out of its case, and keep it out of direct sunlight.
  • The iPhone app warns you when the phone is getting too hot.

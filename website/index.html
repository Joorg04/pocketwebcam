Three steps. No cables.

Your phone and computer find each other automatically on your Wi-Fi.

  1. Install both apps

    Pocket Webcam on your phone (iPhone first, Android in development), and the free Pocket Webcam app on your computer.

  2. Open the app on your phone

    Your computer finds it on the same Wi-Fi network and connects by itself.

  3. Pick “Pocket Webcam”

    Choose it as your camera in any video call, streaming or recording app, or in your browser.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature (all lenses, full resolution, no watermark) is free, with no time limit and no account. If you love it, you’ll be able to leave an optional tip in the app. It unlocks nothing; it just keeps the project going.

Which phones work?

Any iPhone that runs iOS 17 or later. An Android version (Android 10 or later) is in development. Older phones make great dedicated webcams.

Is there an Android version?

It’s in development. It uses the same desktop app and the same protocol as the iPhone version, so it will work with Linux and Windows the same way. Email us if you want to hear when the beta opens.

Does my phone need to stay unlocked?

Yes. Phones only let apps use the camera while they’re open on screen. Pocket Webcam keeps your phone awake while streaming and has a dimmed mode to save battery. Keep it plugged in for long calls.

Does it work over USB?

Not yet. Pocket Webcam works over Wi-Fi, so your phone and computer must be on the same network. A cable option is planned.

What about my Mac?

macOS has Apple’s Continuity Camera built in, which does this for free already. Pocket Webcam focuses on Windows and Linux first.

Where does my video go?

Only to your own computer, directly over your local network. It never passes through our servers, and we don’t collect any data. See the privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

The short version: Pocket Webcam doesn’t collect your data. Your video and sound go from your phone to your own computer, over your own network, and nowhere else.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Pocket Webcam (the iPhone and Android apps, the desktop app, and this website) is made by Nicolai Jørgensen, Norway, who is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: privacy@pocketwebcam.com.

The phone app (iPhone and Android)

Camera

The app uses your camera only while it’s open, to show a preview and to stream video to the Pocket Webcam desktop app you connect it to. The video is encoded on your phone and sent directly to that computer over your local network. It is never sent to us or to any server, and it is not recorded or stored by the app.

Microphone

The app can also send your phone’s microphone to the desktop app, so you can use the phone as a microphone in calls. Your phone asks for your permission the first time a computer wants the microphone. On Android the microphone only works while the app is on screen. While the mic is on, the app shows it, and you can turn it off at any time with the mic button. Like the video, the sound goes directly to your computer over your local network. It is never sent to us or to any server, and it is not recorded or stored by the app.

Local network

To let your computer find your phone, the app announces itself on your local network (using Bonjour on iPhone and Android’s network service discovery on Android). Other devices on the same network can see this announcement, which contains the name you give your phone in the app’s settings, its model type (for example “iPhone16,1” or “Pixel 8”), a random identifier the app made up when you first opened it, and the fingerprint of the phone’s encryption certificate (see below). The identifier and the fingerprint say nothing about you or the phone; they only let your computer recognise the phone, even after you rename it. You can change the name at any time in the app’s settings. The app only answers computers on your local network: it refuses connections that arrive over mobile data.

Pairing and encryption

Your phone and computer pair once, by scanning a code or comparing a 6-digit code. For this, each makes its own encryption key, which never leaves the device (on your phone it’s kept in the iPhone’s Keychain or Android’s KeyStore). After pairing, each remembers the other’s certificate fingerprint and the name it had then. Only paired devices can connect, and everything between them, video and sound included, is encrypted. None of this is sent to us: pairing happens directly between your devices. You can forget a paired computer or phone at any time in the apps’ settings.

Stored on your phone

The app saves your settings (camera, resolution, frame rate, quality, and the device name), the random identifier and the encryption key mentioned above, and the list of paired computers on your phone so they’re remembered next time. The settings never leave your device.

What the app doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No recording or uploading of your video or sound

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your phone’s settings, Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports. They control this; you can turn it off in your phone’s privacy settings (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

If the app offers optional tips, payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

The desktop app

The desktop app runs on your own computer. It receives video from your phone over your local network (and sound, if the phone’s mic is on) and passes it to the virtual webcam and microphone on your computer. Photos and recordings are only made when you ask for them, and are saved on your computer. It doesn’t send any data to us or to anyone else.

Stored on your computer

  • Your settings, and the name and identifier of the phone that connects by itself
  • This computer’s encryption key (readable only by your user account) and the paired phones: their names, identifiers and certificate fingerprints
  • A log of technical messages for finding problems (which phone connected, video sizes, errors; never pictures or sound). It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us when asking for help

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party scripts or fonts. It’s hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The launch list

If you join the launch list, we collect your email address and the two answers you choose (your computer, Linux or Windows, and your phone, iPhone or Android). We use them only to email you when Pocket Webcam launches, and to decide which version to build first. The list is stored with Brevo, our email service provider. You confirm your address through a link in a confirmation email (double opt-in), and every email we send has an unsubscribe link. We delete your data from the list on request. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

If you email us

When you email us (for example at hello@, support@ or privacy@pocketwebcam.com), we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, or, if you asked us to, to tell you when Pocket Webcam launches. Our email is handled by Cloudflare (receiving), Google (our inbox) and Brevo (sending). We delete your messages if you ask us to.

The legal basis for this is your request to us (and, for launch notifications, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying “unsubscribe”).

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we have about you (in practice, only emails you’ve sent us), ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to privacy@pocketwebcam.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Norway that’s Datatilsynet, or the authority in the country where you live.

Children

Pocket Webcam doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what data we collect, we’ll say so clearly in the app before it takes effect.

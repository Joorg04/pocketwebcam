Pocket Webcam vs Camo

Camo is a webcam app with a free plan and a paid Pro plan. Pocket Webcam is newer, completely free, and also runs on Linux. Here’s an honest comparison.

At a glance

Pocket WebcamCamo
PriceFree. Nothing to unlockFree plan, plus a paid Pro plan
ResolutionUp to 4K on supported phones, freeFree: 720p and below. Pro: up to 4K
Frame rateUp to 60 fps or more on supported phones, freeFree: 30 fps. Pro: up to 60 fps
LensesEvery lens, freePro: any lens on your device
ComputersLinux, and Windows 11 (preview). No Mac appmacOS 12.3+ and Windows 10+

Camo details are from Camo’s own website, checked on 2026-11-04. They may have changed since. Spotted a mistake? Email hello@pocketwebcam.com.

Where each one is better

Pocket Webcam

  • Linux. Camo’s desktop app is for macOS and Windows. Pocket Webcam has a Linux app that shows up as a normal V4L2 webcam (setup guide).
  • Full resolution and every lens for free. Camo’s free plan is limited to 720p and 30 fps; higher resolutions, 60 fps and other lenses are part of Pro.
  • Private and simple. No account, and video goes straight from your phone to your computer over your own network.

Camo

  • More platforms today. Camo supports Mac, Windows 10, and Android phones. Pocket Webcam needs Windows 11 and is iPhone-only for now.
  • USB. Camo can work over a cable. Pocket Webcam is Wi-Fi only for now.
  • Further along. Pocket Webcam is new: the iPhone app is in beta and the Windows app is a preview.

Questions

Is Pocket Webcam a free alternative to Camo?

Yes. Pocket Webcam is free with no paid tier, including full resolution and every lens. It works on Linux and Windows 11, but not on a Mac, and only with an iPhone for now.

Does Camo work on Linux?

Camo’s website lists its desktop app for macOS and Windows. Pocket Webcam has a Linux app.

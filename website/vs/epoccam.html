An EpocCam alternative for Windows and Linux

Elgato’s EpocCam page now says that EpocCam is no longer available, and points to Elgato’s Facecam webcams instead. If you used EpocCam to turn your iPhone into a webcam, Pocket Webcam does the same job for free.

Switching from EpocCam

  1. Install the Pocket Webcam desktop app for Windows 11 or Linux.
  2. Install Pocket Webcam on your iPhone (in beta: get an invite).
  3. Open both on the same Wi-Fi, then pick Pocket Webcam as the camera in your call or streaming app.

Pocket Webcam works over Wi-Fi only for now, and the phone’s microphone works on Linux but not yet on Windows (why).

Questions

Is EpocCam discontinued?

Elgato’s EpocCam page says “EpocCam is no longer available” and points to its Facecam webcams instead.

What can I use instead of EpocCam on an iPhone?

Pocket Webcam turns an iPhone into a wireless webcam for Windows 11 and Linux, for free, with every lens and up to 4K. The iPhone app is in beta and the Windows app is a preview.

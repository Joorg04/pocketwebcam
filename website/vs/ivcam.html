Pocket Webcam vs iVCam

iVCam is a phone-as-webcam app for Windows with a free version and a paid license. Pocket Webcam is newer, completely free, and also runs on Linux. Here’s an honest comparison.

Where each one is better

Pocket Webcam

  • No watermark, no ads, no license. Everything is free, on as many computers as you like.
  • Linux. iVCam’s website lists a Windows PC client. Pocket Webcam has a Linux app (setup guide).
  • Private and simple. No account, and video goes straight from your phone to your computer over your own network.

iVCam

  • Older Windows and Android. iVCam supports Windows 7 to 11 and Android phones. Pocket Webcam needs Windows 11 and is iPhone-only for now.
  • USB. iVCam can work over a cable. Pocket Webcam is Wi-Fi only for now.
  • Further along. Pocket Webcam is new: the iPhone app is in beta and the Windows app is a preview.
